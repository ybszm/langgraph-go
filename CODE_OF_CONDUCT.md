# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or
level of contribution.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while remaining precise about technical concerns.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.
- Accept maintainer decisions gracefully when consensus cannot be reached.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sustained disruption, sexualized
attention, and publication of private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile
or repository security contact. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject content
and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

This policy is adapted from the principles of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
